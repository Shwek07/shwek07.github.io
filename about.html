(01)About

A bit more about me

I’m Shakeel, a Software Engineering student at UNASAT in Suriname. I enjoy building things for the web, whether it’s for university, a personal project or a real-world idea. Alongside software development, I also work in graphic design at the university, which has given me a strong interest in both how a product works and how it looks.

ABackground

I started in ICT at Natin-MBO, where I finished the Applicatie Ontwerper programme in 2023. Right after that I continued at Unasat with Software Engineering at HBO level and I am now in my fourth year there.

BWhat I enjoy

One of my favourite parts of development is turning something that would normally take several manual steps into something simple and easy to use.

I also really enjoy front-end development. My design work has a big influence on that. I naturally pay attention to things like spacing, typography, visual hierarchy, animations, hover states and loading states. Those small details can make a website feel much more polished.

CInterests

Outside of my coursework, I spend a lot of time exploring web design and front-end development. I like looking at how modern websites are structured, how they use animation without becoming distracting and what makes a website feel fast and enjoyable to use.

I’m also interested in building small digital tools for local businesses. I like the idea of creating simple software that can take some of that work away and make things easier for both the business and its customers.

DLearning now

I’m still learning and that’s something I actually enjoy about software development. There is always another technology to understand, another problem to solve or another way to improve something I’ve already built.

For now, my goal is simple: keep building, keep learning and become a developer who cares about both the code behind a product and the experience of the person using it.
