(03)The kind of problem I like

Five manual steps. One tap.

My favourite part of development is turning something that takes several manual steps into something simple. Booking a barber used to look like this.

  1. 01Call the shop
  2. 02Wait on hold
  3. 03Ask what is free
  4. 04Write it down
  5. 05Call back to confirm

Booked.

That is what Sranan Kapper and BookInSteps do.
